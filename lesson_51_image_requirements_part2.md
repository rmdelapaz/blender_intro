1. **Action-Oriented Focus:** Heavy emphasis on practical guides, checklists, and timelines to transform learning into doing
2. **Motivation Support:** Multiple images designed to maintain momentum and overcome common psychological barriers

5. **Professional Presentation:** Strong emphasis on portfolio presentation standards and platforms
6. **Problem Prevention:** Multiple warning/comparison images addressing common pitfalls before they happen

### Production Recommendations for Lesson 51

1. **Start with high-priority actionable images** (checklists, timelines, workflows) as these have immediate student impact
2. **Create template-based images first** (commitment contract, description template) as students may want to use/adapt these
3. **Develop motivational images thoughtfully** - they should inspire without feeling cheesy or condescending
4. **Ensure comparison images show meaningful differences** - subtle differences won't register as valuable
5. **Make warning images memorable** (scope creep, tutorial hell) using visual metaphors that stick
6. **Keep celebration/success images authentic** - students have earned genuine recognition
