Welcome to Blender Mastery!

Whether you dream of creating stunning 3D art, animations, game assets, architectural visualizations, or visual effects — you're in the right place. This course takes you from absolute zero to professional competency, step by step.

Blender is the world's leading free and open-source 3D creation suite, used by hobbyists, freelancers, and major studios worldwide. No subscriptions, no limitations — just pure creative power at your fingertips.

What You'll Master

3D Modeling

Master polygon modeling, sculpting, and hard surface techniques. Create anything from characters to props to environments.

Materials & Texturing

Create photorealistic surfaces using PBR materials, UV mapping, and procedural textures in the Shader Editor.

Lighting & Rendering

Master professional lighting techniques and both render engines (Eevee real-time and Cycles path tracing).

Animation

Bring your creations to life with keyframe animation, character rigging, and the Graph Editor.

Simulations

Create dynamic effects with particle systems, cloth simulation, rigid body physics, and more.

Professional Workflows

Learn industry-standard practices, optimization techniques, and portfolio-ready project creation.

What You'll Need

Technical Requirements

  • Blender 4.0+: Free download from blender.org (Windows, Mac, and Linux)
  • Computer: Capable of running Blender (see system requirements below)
  • Mouse: A 3-button mouse is highly recommended (or trackpad with multi-touch)
  • Storage Space: 5–15 GB for Blender, projects, and working files
  • Image Editor (Optional): GIMP (free) or Photoshop for texture work

System Requirements

Minimum: 64-bit quad-core CPU, 8 GB RAM, 1920x1080 display, mouse, OpenGL 4.3 compatible graphics with 2 GB VRAM

Recommended: 64-bit eight-core CPU, 32 GB RAM, 2560x1440 display, 3-button mouse, OpenGL 4.5 compatible graphics with 8 GB+ VRAM

Personal Requirements

  • No Prior Experience Needed: This course starts from absolute zero
  • Patience and Practice: 3D is a journey — embrace the learning process
  • Creative Mindset: Curiosity and willingness to experiment
  • Time Commitment: 45–75 minutes per lesson, at your own pace

Tips for Success

Master the Shortcuts

Blender is keyboard-driven. Learning shortcuts makes you 10x faster. We'll teach them as we go!

Do Every Project

Hands-on projects are where real learning happens. Don't skip them — they build your portfolio too!

Practice Daily

30 minutes daily beats 4-hour weekend marathons. Consistency builds muscle memory and skills.

Experiment Freely

Break things! Try crazy ideas! Ctrl+Z is your friend. Discovery through play is powerful learning.

Save Often (Ctrl+S)

Blender has auto-save, but develop the Ctrl+S habit. Save before experiments and major changes.

Celebrate Progress

Compare your work to last week's, not to pros' work. Every improvement is worth celebrating!
