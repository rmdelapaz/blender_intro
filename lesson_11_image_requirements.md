## Accessibility Considerations

### **Color Blindness**
- Never rely on color alone to convey information
- Use patterns, textures, or labels in addition to color
- Test in grayscale to ensure readability
- Provide sufficient contrast ratios (WCAG AA: 4.5:1 minimum)

### **Alt Text Requirements**
Each image must have descriptive alt text following this formula:
- Describe what the image shows
- Explain what concept it illustrates
- Note key takeaways visible in the image

Example: "Diagram showing three viewing angles (0°, 45°, 90°) of light hitting a surface, with reflection percentages labeled. Demonstrates Fresnel effect where reflection increases from 4% at perpendicular view to 100% at grazing angle."

### **Text Legibility**
- Minimum text size: 12pt in images (14pt preferred)
- High contrast: Dark text on light background or light on dark
- Avoid low-contrast color combinations
- Use clear, readable fonts (no overly decorative typefaces)

### **Diagram Simplicity**
- Limit information density - one concept per diagram
- Use clear visual hierarchy
- Group related elements with proximity and color
- Provide legends/keys where needed
