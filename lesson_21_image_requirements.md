- **Don't rely on color alone:** Use shapes, labels, and patterns in addition to color coding

- **Font size:** Minimum 16px for body text, larger for diagrams
- **Font weight:** Medium to bold on dark backgrounds
- **Contrast:** White or very light text on dark backgrounds
- **Outlines:** Add subtle outlines to text over complex backgrounds
- **Spacing:** Adequate letter and line spacing

- **Simple layouts:** Avoid cluttered compositions in educational diagrams
- **Clear hierarchy:** Most important information largest/brightest
- **Consistent labeling:** Same style across all images
