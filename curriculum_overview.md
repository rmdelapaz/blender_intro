# Blender Mastery Course - Curriculum Overview

## Course Philosophy
This comprehensive Blender course takes students from absolute beginner to professional competency through hands-on, project-based learning. The curriculum emphasizes practical skills, real-world applications, and portfolio building.

## Course Structure

### **INTRODUCTION COURSE** (Modules 1-6: 27 Lessons)
Foundation for all Blender work. Creates confident 3D artists who can model, texture, light, and render professional still images.

### **ADVANCED COURSE** (Modules 7-12: 24 Lessons)
Professional techniques and specialized workflows. Character creation, simulations, advanced node systems, and portfolio projects.

---

## 📖 MODULE BREAKDOWN

### **Module 1: Getting Started with Blender** (4 lessons)
**Goal:** Get comfortable with Blender's interface and basic controls

1. **Welcome to Blender** - Introduction, capabilities, career paths, what makes Blender special
2. **The Blender Interface** - Layout, editors, workspaces, customization, preferences
3. **Navigation and Viewport Control** - 3D viewport navigation, view shortcuts, camera vs view
4. **Basic Object Manipulation** - Transform tools (move, rotate, scale), origin points, snapping

**Module Project:** Navigate confidently, manipulate basic primitives, set up custom workspace

---

### **Module 2: 3D Modeling Fundamentals** (5 lessons)
**Goal:** Master polygon modeling basics and create first 3D objects

5. **Understanding Meshes and Geometry** - Vertices, edges, faces, normals, mesh topology
6. **Edit Mode Essentials** - Selection modes, basic operations (extrude, inset, bevel), proportional editing
7. **Modeling Your First Object** - Complete mug/cup project, loop cuts, edge flow
8. **Modifiers Introduction** - Array, mirror, solidify, subdivision surface modifiers
9. **Precision Modeling Techniques** - Measurements, snap tools, constraints, reference images

**Module Project:** Model a complete still life scene (table, objects, props) with proper topology

---

### **Module 3: Materials and Texturing** (5 lessons)
**Goal:** Create photorealistic surfaces and understand PBR workflow

10. **Introduction to Shader Editor** - Node basics, BSDF shaders, connecting nodes
11. **PBR Materials Explained** - Physically Based Rendering, base color, metallic, roughness
12. **UV Unwrapping Basics** - UV maps, seams, unwrap methods, UV editing
13. **Texture Painting** - Texture Paint workspace, brushes, baking textures
14. **Procedural Textures** - Noise, voronoi, wave textures, mixing techniques, ColorRamp

**Module Project:** Create 5 realistic materials (wood, metal, plastic, glass, fabric) and apply to objects

---

### **Module 4: Lighting and Rendering** (5 lessons)
**Goal:** Master lighting techniques and both render engines

15. **Understanding Light Types** - Point, sun, spot, area lights, light properties
16. **Three-Point Lighting Setup** - Key, fill, rim lighting, studio lighting
17. **HDRI and World Lighting** - Environment textures, outdoor lighting, rotation tricks
18. **Eevee Real-time Rendering** - Eevee settings, screen space effects, render properties
19. **Cycles Path Tracing** - Cycles vs Eevee, samples, denoising, render optimization

**Module Project:** Light and render the same scene in both Eevee and Cycles, compare results

---

### **Module 5: Camera and Composition** (4 lessons)
**Goal:** Create compelling shots with professional camera work

20. **Camera Basics** - Camera types, focal length, sensor size, depth of field
21. **Composition Principles** - Rule of thirds, leading lines, framing, negative space
22. **Depth of Field and Focus** - Aperture, F-stop, focus distance, realistic DOF
23. **Camera Animation** - Keyframing cameras, camera constraints, follow paths

**Module Project:** Create 3 different compositions of the same scene showcasing composition rules

---

### **Module 6: Introduction to Animation** (4 lessons)
**Goal:** Understand animation basics and bring objects to life

24. **Animation Fundamentals** - Keyframes, interpolation, timing, spacing, principles of animation
25. **The Timeline and Keyframes** - Dope sheet, inserting keyframes, auto-keyframe, frame ranges
26. **Graph Editor Essentials** - F-curves, handles, easing, animation modifiers
27. **Basic Character Animation** - Simple character rigs, pose-to-pose animation, walk cycles

**Module Project:** Animate a complete 5-10 second scene with camera movement and object animation

---

## 🎓 ADVANCED COURSE MODULES (Coming Soon)

### **Module 7: Advanced Modeling** (4 lessons)
- Sculpting Basics (Sculpt Mode, brushes, remeshing, Dyntopo)
- Hard Surface Modeling (Boolean operations, bevels, edge flow for mechanical parts)
- Retopology Fundamentals (Clean topology, retopo tools, mesh optimization)
- Advanced Modifier Stack (Complex modifier combinations, non-destructive workflows)

**Project:** Create a detailed character bust through sculpting, then retopologize

---

### **Module 8: Particles and Simulations** (4 lessons)
- Particle Systems Overview (Emitters, hair particles, instancing)
- Hair and Fur (Hair particle system, grooming, children particles)
- Cloth Simulation (Cloth modifier, collision, pinning, wind)
- Rigid Body Physics (Active/passive objects, constraints, explosions)

**Project:** Create dynamic scene with cloth, hair, and physics simulations

---

### **Module 9: Character Creation** (4 lessons)
- Character Modeling Workflow (Reference images, proportions, edge flow)
- Armature and Bones (Bone creation, parenting, bone constraints)
- Weight Painting (Influence maps, automatic weights, troubleshooting)
- Rigging Essentials (IK/FK, control bones, character rig setup)

**Project:** Model, rig, and pose a complete character

---

### **Module 10: Node Systems Mastery** (4 lessons)
- Geometry Nodes Introduction (Node basics, modifiers, attributes)
- Procedural Modeling with Nodes (Instance on points, scatter, distributions)
- Advanced Shader Techniques (Complex node trees, realistic materials, tricks)
- Compositor Basics (Render layers, color grading, effects)

**Project:** Create procedural assets and composite a final render with effects

---

### **Module 11: Professional Workflows** (4 lessons)
- Scene Organization (Collections, outliner, linking/appending, scene management)
- Optimization Techniques (Poly count, LODs, texture optimization, rendering speed)
- Rendering for Production (Render farms, render layers, cryptomatte, passes)
- Post-Processing (Compositor workflows, color grading, final touches)

**Project:** Optimize and render a complex scene with professional pipeline

---

### **Module 12: Portfolio Projects** (4 lessons)
- Product Visualization Project (Commercial product render, studio lighting, reflections)
- Architectural Visualization (Interior/exterior architectural scene, realistic materials)
- Character Animation Showcase (Rigged character in short animation with story)
- Your Portfolio Piece (Student choice: original project showcasing all skills)

**Final Project:** Complete portfolio-ready piece in your chosen specialization

## 🎨 Teaching Methodology

### Lesson Structure
Each lesson follows a proven structure:
1. **Learning Objectives** - Clear goals with estimated time
2. **Sticky Table of Contents** - Always-visible navigation
3. **Introduction** - Why this matters, real-world context
4. **Core Concepts** - Main teaching with visual aids (Mermaid diagrams, screenshots)
5. **Demonstrations** - Step-by-step practical examples
6. **Hands-On Exercise** - Apply concepts immediately
7. **Common Mistakes** - Troubleshooting and tips
8. **Summary** - Key takeaways and next steps
9. **Navigation** - Clear path to previous/next lessons

### Pedagogical Approach
- **Learning by Doing:** Every lesson includes hands-on practice
- **Progressive Complexity:** Each lesson builds on previous knowledge
- **Visual Learning:** Diagrams, screenshots, and visual explanations
- **Real-World Analogies:** Complex concepts explained through familiar ideas
- **Encouragement:** Friendly, supportive tone throughout
- **Portfolio Building:** Projects designed to showcase in portfolio

---

## 💡 Student Success Tips

1. **Follow Sequential Order** - Each lesson builds on previous ones
2. **Master Shortcuts** - Keyboard shortcuts make you 10x faster
3. **Complete Every Project** - Hands-on work is where learning happens
4. **Practice Daily** - 30 minutes daily > 4 hours weekly
5. **Save Often** - Develop Ctrl+S muscle memory
6. **Experiment Freely** - Break things, learn from mistakes
7. **Join Community** - Blender communities are incredibly helpful
8. **Build Portfolio** - Document and showcase your best work
9. **Take Breaks** - Rest prevents burnout and improves learning
10. **Celebrate Progress** - Compare to your past work, not others'
